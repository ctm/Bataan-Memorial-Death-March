# 2027 Bataan Memorial Death March Training Log

I do this every year.  There's more info in [the top-level
README.md](README.md) (although some of it may be outdated, due to me
having less time than normal).  My goal for 2027 is to be competitive
at the [Bataan Memorial Death March](https://bataanmarch.com/) and to
continue inspiring others.

### Registration opens October 1st

As of September 11th, 2026, the [BMDM page](https://bataanmarch.com/)
says that registration opens October 1st.  BMDM 2027 will be held on
Saturday, March 20th, 2027.  I'll update this page once I've
registered.

### Survivors

On August 15th, 2026, [James
Bollich](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7QKQAZqZWrU) turned 105. As
far as I know, James is still alive and is probably the last living
survivor of the Bataan Death March.  James was one of two speakers at
BMDM 2019 (the last in person BMDM before the three year covid
hiatus).
